# Security policy

This policy covers every public repository in the [Flowella](https://github.com/Flowella) organisation and the Flowella service at [app.flowella.io](https://app.flowella.io).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not report security vulnerabilities in public GitHub issues, discussions or pull requests.

Email [security@flowella.io](mailto:security@flowella.io) with:

- a description of the issue and its potential impact
- the affected repository, file, URL or version
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- your name or handle, if you would like to be credited

We will acknowledge your report, keep you informed while we investigate, and let you know when the issue is resolved. Please give us a reasonable amount of time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.

## Out of scope

- Vulnerabilities in third-party services we integrate with, such as WhatsApp, Meta, HubSpot or n8n. Please report those to the vendor directly.
- Findings from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact.
- Social engineering, phishing or physical attacks against Flowella staff or customers.
